## Before you present: checklist

- [ ] Every statement is true. "Make the truth good, then just tell it." [convince]
- [ ] One person talks. [investors]
- [ ] Few words on slides. [investors]
- [ ] No long detours into business model or secondary matters. [investors]
- [ ] No credentials standing in for evidence. [X 2026-08-03](https://x.com/paulg/status/2084094594766327890)
- [ ] You know what you'll ask for, and you'll ask what happens next. [fr]

## Filled-in example (invented)

1. *We answer engineers' questions about their own codebase, in Slack.*
2. Forty teams use it daily. One team lead stopped running weekly onboarding sessions for new hires.
3. $6k MRR, growing 6% a week for the last eight weeks; 85% of teams active after a month.
4. Why now: code-reading models became good enough in the last year. Hops: startups' engineering teams, then
   mid-size companies, then every company with an internal codebase.
5. Competitor A only searches docs; B needs a self-hosted install; C answers general coding questions, not
   your code.
6. Two founders who built the internal tools team at their last job.
7. *Your codebase, answered in Slack.*
