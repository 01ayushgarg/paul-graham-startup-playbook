# 09 · Great work and ambition

## Choose the work

> "The work you choose needs to have three qualities: it has to be something you have a natural aptitude
> for, that you have a deep interest in, and that offers scope to do great work." [greatwork, Jul 2023]

> "Four steps: choose a field, learn enough to get to the frontier, notice gaps, explore promising ones."
> [greatwork]

> "What are you excessively curious about — curious to a degree that would bore most other people? That's
> what you're looking for." [greatwork]

> "When in doubt, optimize for interestingness." [greatwork]

> "The way to figure out what to work on is by working. If you're not sure what to work on, guess."
> [greatwork]

## Superlinear returns

> "If your product is only half as good as your competitor's, you don't get half as many customers. You get
> no customers, and you go out of business." [superlinear, Oct 2023]

> "They reduce to two fundamental causes: exponential growth and thresholds." [superlinear]

> "The most obvious one is to seek work that compounds." "Always be learning." [superlinear]

> "If you come across something that's mediocre yet still popular, it could be a good idea to replace it."
> [superlinear]

> "Take as much risk as you can afford; if you're not failing occasionally you're probably being too
> conservative." [superlinear]

## Consistency, earnestness, effort

> "People who do great things don't get a lot done every day. They get something done, rather than
> nothing." [greatwork]

> "The core of being earnest is being intellectually honest." [greatwork]

> "Be aggressively willing to admit that you're mistaken." [greatwork]

> "There are three ingredients in great work: natural ability, practice, and effort." [hwh, Jun 2021]

> "Many problems have a hard core at the center, surrounded by easier stuff at the edges. Working hard means
> aiming toward the center to the extent you can." [hwh]

> "If you're working hard but not getting good enough results, you should switch." [hwh]

## Procrastinate on the right things

> "So the question is not how to avoid procrastination, but how to procrastinate well." [procrastination,
> Dec 2005]

> "Unless you're working on the biggest things you could be working on, you're type-B procrastinating, no
> matter how much you're getting done." [procrastination]

> "What's the best thing you could be working on, and why aren't you?" [procrastination]

## Own it, love it, aim high

> "Working on a project of your own is as different from ordinary work as skating is from walking."
> [own, Jun 2021]

> "The test of whether people love what they do is whether they'd do it even if they weren't paid for it."
> [love, Jan 2006]

> "Prestige is just fossilized inspiration." [love]

> "With ambition, you tend to get one step below what you aim for. So unless you try to be the best, you
> won't even be good." [X 2022-12-20](https://x.com/paulg/status/1605321011012026369)

## Where you live

> "Great cities attract ambitious people... the city sends you a message: you could do more; you should try
> harder." [cities, May 2008] In his reading: New York says make more money, Cambridge says be smarter,
> Silicon Valley says be more powerful. [cities]

**Checks to run:**
1. Aptitude, interest, scope: score your current work on each.
2. Does this work compound? What are you learning this month?
3. What's the hard core of your problem, and how much time is going to the edges?
4. What's the best thing you could be working on, and why aren't you?
