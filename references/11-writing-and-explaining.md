# 11 · Writing and explaining

Founders write all the time: the pitch, the website, the investor update, the one-sentence description.
PG treats writing as thinking.

> "Good writing is an illusion: what people call good writing is actually good thinking."
> [X 2022-07-31](https://x.com/paulg/status/1553709144279896064)

> "If AI saves people from having to write, it will also save them from having the ideas that writing
> engenders." [X 2023-01-05](https://x.com/paulg/status/1610812670290325504)

## Write like you talk [talk, Oct 2015]

> "Here's a simple trick for getting more people to read what you write: write in spoken language."

> "You don't need complex sentences to express complex ideas."

> "Is this the way I'd say this if I were talking to a friend?" If it isn't, imagine what you would say, and
> use that instead.

> "After writing the first draft, try explaining to a friend what you just wrote. Then replace the draft
> with what you said to your friend."

## Write simply [simply, Mar 2021]

> "The less energy they expend on your prose, the more they'll have left for your ideas."

> "Whereas writing simply keeps you honest. If you say nothing simply, it will be obvious to everyone,
> including you."

> "I write the first draft fast, then spend days editing it... Much of this editing is cutting."

## The startup version

> "A startup should be able to explain in one or two sentences exactly what it does... You probably
> shouldn't even start a company to do something that can't be described compellingly in one or two
> sentences." [startuplessons, Apr 2006]

> "Many investors explicitly use that as a test, reasoning (correctly) that if you can't explain your plans
> concisely, you don't really understand them." [convince, Aug 2013]

> "The most important component of writing clearly is simply to have high standards for clarity."
> [X 2026-06-02](https://x.com/paulg/status/2061842652765761682)

> "Before writing an essay, have ChatGPT write one on the same topic to show you what would be the
> conventional thing to say, so you can avoid saying that." [X 2023-01-26](https://x.com/paulg/status/1618676092163997696)

**Checks to run on any founder writing:**
1. Can you say what the company does in one or two sentences a friend would understand?
2. Read it aloud. Where does it clank?
3. Which sentences could be cut without losing an idea? Cut them.
