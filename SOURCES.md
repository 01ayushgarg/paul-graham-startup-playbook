# Sources

Everything in this repo comes from Paul Graham's own writing: his essays on paulgraham.com and his posts on
X ([@paulg](https://x.com/paulg)). All accessed on **6 October 2026**.

## How citations work

- `[slug]` = the essay at `https://www.paulgraham.com/slug.html`. Dates are the essay's own date line.
- `[slug, note n]` = footnote n of that essay.
- `[X yyyy-mm-dd]` = his post on X from that date (linked where cited; full list below).
- Quotes are copied from the essays' raw text and were checked by script against it. Footnote markers like
  [1] are left out, and line breaks are joined.
- When PG credits an idea to someone else (Paul Buchheit, Joe Kraus, Sam Altman, Peter Thiel, Trevor
  Blackwell and others), the playbook says so.
- Numbers are as of the essay's date (for example, fundraising amounts from 2013).
